## QUORA
**Answer this question:** What is the one baby product worth spending money on for a newborn, and which popular gadgets are overrated?

As a NICU nurse and a mother of three, I'd answer this with one question: does it help your baby sleep safely, and does it help you sleep at all? If the answer is yes, it's worth the money. If not, it can wait.

Here's how that plays out in practice.

Worth prioritizing: a firm, flat sleep surface that meets current safety standards, with nothing else in it. No pillows, bumpers or loose blankets. Next, a well-fitting swaddle or sleep sack, since it keeps a newborn comfortable without loose bedding. Then a simple way to check on your baby at night without turning on bright lights.

A smart bassinet can also be a real help. I rented one for my youngest in spring 2024, and on night four at 3:10 a.m. I watched it settle her with no effort from me. I'll admit I got emotional. Renting first is a good idea, because you find out whether it suits your baby before you commit hundreds of dollars.

Often overrated: gadgets that promise to monitor vital signs, like sock sensors that report oxygen or heart rate. In my experience they can create more worry than reassurance. A loose fit or a wiggly baby triggers a false alarm, and a frightened parent ends up in the ER in the middle of the night. They aren't a substitute for safe sleep habits or for your pediatrician's advice.

One caveat: every baby is different, and if your newborn has a medical condition, talk to your care team before choosing any monitoring device.
